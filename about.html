The studio

A desk that treats house-hunting like a design brief.

Lumina Living started as a notebook of houses Amina Hassan wanted to remember: which rooms held the afternoon, which gardens still smelled of rain, which towers were honest about the service charge.

The desk is still small. The live catalogue on this site has thirteen sample homes, filed by mood as well as by price. Nairobi, the coast, and Nanyuki sit on the same shelf so a family can compare a Karen garden with a Nyali breeze without losing the plot.

This website is a front-end demonstration. The homes, prices, and people are sample content. Forms stay in your browser. They do not email a real office.

Clear prices

Asking figures stay on the card. Extra costs, including stamp duty and legal fees, get named before anyone falls in love with a pool.

Local guides
